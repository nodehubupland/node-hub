PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

1. About Node Hub

Node Hub is a community-built directory for discovering Upland Nodes and their communities. This policy explains what information may be collected when you use the website and how that information is used.

2. Information we collect

When you create a Node Hub account, we may collect your email address, username and authentication information. When you submit a Node, we may collect the Node information you provide, such as its name, description, city, country, neighborhood, continent, logo and community links.

3. How we use information

We use account information to authenticate users, provide access to dashboards, manage Node submissions and communicate about account or support matters. Node information is used to operate the public Node Hub directory and display approved Nodes and their community information.

4. Public Node information

Information submitted for a Node may become publicly visible after review and approval. This can include the Node name, description, location, logo and official community links such as Discord, Telegram and X.

5. Authentication and service providers

Node Hub uses Supabase for authentication and database services. Authentication information is processed through Supabase services. We do not sell personal information.

6. Cookies and local storage

Node Hub may use browser local storage to remember preferences such as the selected language and to maintain application state. The website may also use essential authentication mechanisms provided by its service providers.

7. Passwords

Node Hub does not directly store your password in the website code. Password authentication and password recovery are handled by the authentication service.

8. Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by the Node Hub platform. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Your choices

You may contact Node Hub to request help with your account or questions about information associated with your Node Hub account.

10. Contact

For privacy or account questions, contact nodehubupland@gmail.com.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Node Hub evolves. The latest version will always be available on this page.
